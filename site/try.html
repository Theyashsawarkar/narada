How it works

    Colour means something

    Accent: where you are. The picked row, the focused pane, the open place, the caret.
    Green: done, or alive. Sent, approved, online, synced, NORMAL mode.
    Yellow: waiting. Undo window, scheduled, awaiting your approval, queued while offline.
    Red: danger or failure. Not sent, blocked, delete confirm, offline.
    Peach: look here. Your mentions, search matches, the "new messages" line, attachments.
    Blue: read (✓✓), links, INSERT mode.

    About this demo

    Everything here is made up: the people, the mail, the chats. It runs in this page and sends nothing anywhere.

    Here Claude's drafts are written ahead of time. In narada, A runs Claude (claude -p, your own login) on the chat or mail you have open, so it writes in the language and tone you use with that person, and nothing goes out until you press y.

    The real thing is a terminal program: Gmail, Google Chat and WhatsApp in one keyboard-driven screen. Install it.

    Keys to start with

    • j k move, ⏎ opens, h goes back
    • 1–4 or H L: Mail, Chat, WhatsApp, AI requests
    • i writes, ⏎ sends, u takes it back while it waits
    • A Claude writes it: y use · e edit · c change · n drop
    • Ctrl+g while writing: Claude polishes what you typed
    • Space the menu, / search everything, ? every key
